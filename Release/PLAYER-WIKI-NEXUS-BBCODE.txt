[size=5][b]Fishing Expanded — Every Fish in Stardew Valley Will Remember You[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]English[/b][/size]

Every fish in Stardew Valley is starting to remember you—and form an opinion about you.
Fishing Expanded turns the fishing minigame into a fair, deep, and endlessly addictive career. It doesn't overturn vanilla mechanics, doesn't add fake items, and doesn't cheat.
It feels like a true DLC: it respects vanilla fishing first, then makes it look like this is exactly how the game was always meant to be.

[size=4][b]Core Gameplay: Fish Reputation — Every Species Rates You[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Every species keeps its own independent [b]opinion of you[/b], from -10 to 100 — your reputation within that fish population.
[*]Low reputation: they barely take you seriously — weak, easy catches. High reputation: they start testing you properly — stronger fish, far better rewards.
[*]A successful catch raises their opinion of you; an escape lowers it.
[*][b]The five legendary fish stand above all opinions[/b], keeping their vanilla experience; catching one once grants a crown directly.
[/list]

[i]In short: respect is earned dynamically. The more a species respects you, the harder it tests you — and the more it gives back.[/i]

[size=4][b]How Does Their Opinion of You Rise After a Successful Catch?[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Perfect catches (no escapes)[/b] impress them the most.
[*]The more escapes, the less impressed they are.
[*][b]The higher your fishing level, the faster their opinion of you grows[/b] (max fishing level is fastest).
[*]Every successful catch is worth at least +1 respect.
[/list]

[size=4][b]What Changes as Their Opinion of You Grows[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Harder tests[/b]: highly-rated fish become significantly harder and may use tricks like jumping.
[*][b]More fish[/b]: catch quantities increase; at opinion 100 you can catch up to 100 at once.
[*][b]Better quality[/b]: opinion 10 guarantees silver, opinion 25 guarantees gold, and opinion 50 guarantees iridium.
[*][b]More XP[/b]: highly-rated fish are great for fishing mastery.
[*][b]Bigger fish[/b]: held fish, flying animations, and displays all become larger.
[*][b]Negative reputation[/b]: fish go easy on you and the catch bar gets more protection, great for learning.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Crowns: More Than a Collection[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Normal fish grant a [b]crown[/b] in the collection page after a successful catch while their [b]opinion of you is high[/b].
[*]The 5 vanilla legendary fish grant a crown [b]directly on the first catch[/b].
[*]Crowns have a real effect: [b]your rod handling improves[/b].
[list]
[*]The catch bar becomes more responsive, smoother, and less floaty.
[*]More crowns mean better handling.
[*]Countable crowns come from [b]56 normal vanilla fish + 5 vanilla legendary fish[/b]; collecting all 61 gives the ultimate feel.
[/list]
[*]Reach opinion 95+ and succeed with [b]challenge bait[/b] to upgrade the crown into a [b]flowing golden crown[/b]—purely gorgeous and a great flex. (At opinion 100 + challenge bait, there is even an easter egg.)
[/list]

[i]Other mod fish / special entries only display a star as a collection badge; they do not affect rod handling.[/i]

[size=4][b]What Happens When Your Reputation Is High?[/b][/size]

Once a species rates you highly, you will meet truly powerful fish: the champions they send can reach a theoretical power of about 5000 (for reference, vanilla legendary fish are usually below 100), with fiercer acceleration and even full-screen leaps.
But the rewards scale up too—catch quantity, XP, and quality all increase significantly. You will never be asked to “fight harder for less.”

[list]
[*][b]Fish can jump[/b]: they leap up and down, requiring faster reactions.
[*][b]Fish get tired[/b]: the longer the battle, the slower the fish becomes—but your rewards do not decrease.
[*][b]Truce breaks[/b]: if you hold the rod still for 3 seconds, the fish stops to rest and gives you breathing room.
[*][b]Backseeding[/b]: the same species at the same opinion level keeps the same behavior until you finally break it, so you can learn and practice.
[*][b]Fish assistance[/b]: fish you have already conquered may assist you in battle, greatly extending your catch bar.
[*][b]Challenge Bait = Harder Boss Fights[/b]: only at high difficulty, the vanilla challenge bait system is reworked:
[list]
[*]The fish no longer gets tired. No assistance.
[*]Catching within 5 minutes grants extra quantity.
[*]After the time limit, stars drop and the catch shrinks, but you [b]can still earn the crown[/b]—it is not all or nothing.
[/list]
[*][b]Consecutive failures show mercy[/b]: high-reputation fish escape more slowly in the low catch-bar zone, giving you more chances to learn.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Even Failure Has Consolation Prizes[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Battles over [b]30 seconds[/b]: chance to receive a +3 fishing dish.
[*]Battles over [b]60 seconds[/b]: chance to receive [b]Seafoam Pudding[/b] (+4 fishing).
[/list]

[i]Your effort is never wasted; patience pays off.[/i]

[b]A Giant Appears—The Whole Village Stares.[/b]
At high levels, you can catch absurdly huge fish. Nearby villagers will gasp in awe (even some special villagers~). But show them off early—once you enter your farmhouse, that giant fish only lives on in legend (and in the size record).

[size=4][b]Starfruit Tea (Late-Game Bonus)[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Once a species' opinion of you reaches [b]50+[/b], successful catches have a chance to drop [b]Starfruit Tea[/b].
[*]The higher their opinion, the higher the chance; around 25% at opinion 100.
[*]Starfruit Tea permanently increases max energy—a genuinely valuable reward.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Trash, Algae, and Crab Pots[/b][/size]

[list]

[*]At opinion 8, each catch gives up to 8 items.
[*]Crab pot hauls also count as a “success,” but each pot can only be collected once per day, so it is naturally limited.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Quick Start Guide[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]New players[/b]: first build positive reputation with common fish while raising your fishing level.
[*][b]Mid-game[/b]: collect crowns to improve rod handling.
[*][b]Going for reputation 100[/b]: recommended with all crowns + max fishing level + 2x Trap Bobbers + Iridium Seafoam Pudding.
[*][b]Want giant fish[/b]: raise a species' opinion to 8+, then parade it around town before going home.
[*][b]Want Starfruit Tea[/b]: focus on building opinion 50+ with your favorite species.
[*][b]Want a real challenge[/b]: equip Challenge Bait and start the backseeding boss fight.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Fishing Expanded — 让星露谷的每一条鱼都记住你[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]中文[/b][/size]

星露谷的每一条鱼，都开始记得你——也开始对你有了评价。
Fishing Expanded 把钓鱼小游戏做成一段公平、耐玩、越钓越上头的完整生涯。不推翻原有机制，没有凭空造物，没有作弊。
它像一个真正的 DLC：先完全尊重原版钓鱼，再让它看起来本来就该是这样。

[size=4][b]核心玩法：鱼群评价——每个种群都在给你打分[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]每一个鱼种都对你持有一份独立的[b]评价[/b]（-10 ~ 100），这就是你在该鱼群中的声誉。
[*]评价低：它们没把你当回事——鱼更弱、更容易钓；评价高：它们开始认真考你——鱼更强、奖励更好。
[*]成功钓起会让它们更看得起你（评价上涨），脱杆则会让评价下跌。
[*][b]五大传奇鱼超然于一切评价之外[/b]，保持原版体验；钓到一次直接给皇冠。
[/list]

[i]总之：尊重是动态挣来的。一个鱼种越看得起你，就越要认真考你——给的也越多。[/i]

[size=4][b]一次成功，鱼群的评价怎么涨？[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]完美钓起（没脱杆）[/b]最让它们刮目相看。
[*]脱杆次数越多，它们越不为所动。
[*][b]你的钓鱼等级越高，它们对你的评价涨得越快[/b]（满级钓鱼等级最快）。
[*]每次成功至少涨一点尊重，保底不落空。
[/list]

[size=4][b]评价升高后的变化[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]考验更难[/b]：高评价鱼种会明显变强，还会出现“跳鱼”等花招。
[*][b]鱼更多[/b]：钓到的数量会变多，评价 100 时一次最多 100 条。
[*][b]品质更好[/b]：评价 10 保底银星、25 保底金星、50 保底铱星。
[*][b]经验更多[/b]：高评价鱼刷钓鱼精通特别快。
[*][b]鱼更大[/b]：钓上来后拿在手里、飞行动画、展示都会变大。
[*][b]负数评价[/b]：鱼会放水，蓄力槽保护更强，适合新手练手。
[/list]

[size=4][b]皇冠：不只是收藏[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]普通鱼在[b]高难度成功钓起[/b]后，图鉴会获得[b]皇冠[/b]。
[*]原版 5 条传奇鱼[b]钓到一次直接给皇冠[/b]。
[*]皇冠的作用是实实在在的：[b]鱼竿手感变好[/b]。
[list]
[*]绿条更跟手、更顺滑、不拖泥带水。
[*]皇冠越多，手感越好。
[*]可计数皇冠来自 [b]56 种普通原生鱼 + 5 条原版传奇鱼[/b]，集齐这 61 个 = 完全体手感。
[/list]
[*]评价达到 95 以上，再用[b]挑战鱼饵[/b]成功钓起，皇冠会升级成[b]流动金色皇冠[/b]，纯粹好看又能秀。（评价 100 + 挑战鱼饵，还有彩蛋）
[/list]

[i]其他 Mod 鱼/特殊条目只显示“星星”，作为收藏；不影响手感。[/i]

[size=4][b]声誉很高时会发生什么？[/b][/size]

当一个鱼种对你评价极高，你会遇见真正强大的对手：它们派出的战力理论上可达约 5000（相对而言，原版鱼王一般在 100 以内）、加速更凶，甚至能整段横跳。
但奖励同步拉满——鱼获、经验、品质大幅增强。你永远不会被要求“打得更难，拿得更少”。

[list]
[*][b]鱼会跳[/b]：上蹿下跳，需要更快反应。
[*][b]鱼会累[/b]：战斗时间越长，鱼会慢慢变慢，但你的奖励不减少。
[*][b]可以停战[/b]：你 3 秒不动鱼竿，鱼会停下来休息，给你喘口气的时间。
[*][b]背板机制[/b]：同一种鱼、同一档评价，在你成功破招之前，行为会固定。可以记招、练招。
[*][b]鱼的协助[/b]：已经收入麾下的鱼，有一定概率战斗中协助，大幅增加你的钓鱼条长度。
[*][b]挑战鱼饵 = 更难的 Boss 战[/b]：仅对高评价的鱼，重做原版挑战鱼饵机制：
[list]
[*]鱼不再会累。不再有协助。
[*]5 分钟内钓到有额外数量加成。
[*]超时后星星会掉，鱼获变少，但[b]仍有机会拿到皇冠[/b]，不会血本无归。
[/list]
[*][b]连续失败会“放水”[/b]：高评价的鱼在低条区逃跑速度会变慢，给你更多熟悉机会。
[/list]

[size=4][b]失败了也有安慰奖[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]战斗超过 [b]30 秒[/b]：有概率获得 +3 钓鱼料理。
[*]战斗超过 [b]60 秒[/b]：有概率获得 [b]海泡布丁[/b]（钓鱼 +4）。
[/list]

[i]肝不会白肝，耐心有回报。[/i]

[b]巨物登场，全村围观。[/b]
高等级之后，你能钓起大到离谱的鱼：附近村民不禁赞叹（甚至包括一些特殊村民~）不过要炫耀就得趁早——一旦走进农舍，这条巨鱼就只存在于传说（和尺寸记录）里了。

[list]

[*]评价越高概率越高，评价 100 时约 25%。
[*]星之果茶可以永久提高体力上限，是实打实的好东西。
[/list]
