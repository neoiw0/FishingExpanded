[size=5][b]Fishing Expanded — Every Fish in Stardew Valley Will Remember You[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]English[/b][/size]

Every fish in Stardew Valley is starting to remember you—and form an opinion about you.
Fishing Expanded turns the fishing minigame into a fair, deep, and endlessly addictive career. It doesn't overturn vanilla mechanics, doesn't add fake items, and doesn't cheat.
It feels like a true DLC: it respects vanilla fishing first, then makes it look like this is exactly how the game was always meant to be.

[size=4][b]Core Gameplay: Fish Difficulty Levels[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Every fish has its own independent difficulty level (-10 to 100).
[*]At low levels, fish are weaker and easier to catch; at high levels, they are stronger and more rewarding.
[*]A successful catch raises the level; a failed catch lowers it.
[*][b]The five legendary fish are exempt from this system[/b], keeping their vanilla experience; catching one once grants a crown directly.
[/list]

[i]In short: difficulty dynamically balances. The fish get stronger, and so do you.[/i]

[size=4][b]How Does the Fish Difficulty Level Increase After a Successful Catch?[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Perfect catches (no escapes)[/b] gain the most.
[*]The more escapes, the less you gain.
[*][b]The higher your fishing level, the faster your fish levels grow[/b] (max fishing level is fastest).
[*]Every successful catch has a minimum gain of +1.
[/list]

[size=4][b]What Changes as Your Fish Levels Up[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Harder fish[/b]: high-level fish become significantly harder and may use tricks like jumping.
[*][b]More fish[/b]: catch quantities increase; at level 100 you can catch up to 100 at once.
[*][b]Better quality[/b]: level 10 guarantees silver, level 25 guarantees gold, and level 50 guarantees iridium.
[*][b]More XP[/b]: high-level fish are great for fishing mastery.
[*][b]Bigger fish[/b]: held fish, flying animations, and displays all become larger.
[*][b]Negative levels[/b]: fish become weaker and the catch bar gets more protection, great for learning.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Crowns: More Than a Collection[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Normal fish earn a [b]crown[/b] in the collection page after a successful catch at [b]high difficulty[/b].
[*]The 5 vanilla legendary fish grant a crown [b]directly on the first catch[/b].
[*]Crowns have a real effect: [b]your rod handling improves[/b].
[list]
[*]The catch bar becomes more responsive, smoother, and less floaty.
[*]More crowns mean better handling.
[*]Countable crowns come from [b]56 normal vanilla fish + 5 vanilla legendary fish[/b]; collecting all 61 gives the ultimate feel.
[/list]
[*]Reach level 95+ and succeed with [b]challenge bait[/b] to upgrade the crown into a [b]flowing golden crown[/b]—purely gorgeous and a great flex. (At level 100 + challenge bait, there is even an easter egg.)
[/list]

[i]Other mod fish / special entries only display a star as a collection badge; they do not affect rod handling.[/i]

[size=4][b]What Happens at High Difficulty?[/b][/size]

At high levels, you will meet truly powerful fish: theoretical difficulty can reach about 5000 (for reference, vanilla legendary fish are usually below 100), with fiercer acceleration and even full-screen leaps.
But the rewards scale up too—catch quantity, XP, and quality all increase significantly. You will never be asked to “fight harder for less.”

[list]
[*][b]Fish can jump[/b]: they leap up and down, requiring faster reactions.
[*][b]Fish get tired[/b]: the longer the battle, the slower the fish becomes—but your rewards do not decrease.
[*][b]Truce breaks[/b]: if you hold the rod still for 3 seconds, the fish stops to rest and gives you breathing room.
[*][b]Backseeding[/b]: the same fish at the same level keeps the same behavior until you finally break it, so you can learn and practice.
[*][b]Fish assistance[/b]: fish you have already conquered may assist you in battle, greatly extending your catch bar.
[*][b]Challenge Bait = Harder Boss Fights[/b]: only at high difficulty, the vanilla challenge bait system is reworked:
[list]
[*]The fish no longer gets tired. No assistance.
[*]Catching within 5 minutes grants extra quantity.
[*]After the time limit, stars drop and the catch shrinks, but you [b]can still earn the crown[/b]—it is not all or nothing.
[/list]
[*][b]Consecutive failures show mercy[/b]: high-difficulty fish escape more slowly in the low catch-bar zone, giving you more chances to learn.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Even Failure Has Consolation Prizes[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Battles over [b]30 seconds[/b]: chance to receive a +3 fishing dish.
[*]Battles over [b]60 seconds[/b]: chance to receive [b]Seafoam Pudding[/b] (+4 fishing).
[/list]

[i]Your effort is never wasted; patience pays off.[/i]

[b]A Giant Appears—The Whole Village Stares.[/b]
At high levels, you can catch absurdly huge fish. Nearby villagers will gasp in awe (even some special villagers~). But show them off early—once you enter your farmhouse, that giant fish only lives on in legend (and in the size record).

[size=4][b]Quick Start Guide[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]New players[/b]: first raise common fish to positive levels while raising your fishing level.
[*][b]Mid-game[/b]: collect crowns to improve rod handling.
[*][b]Going for level 100[/b]: recommended with all crowns + max fishing level + 2x Trap Bobbers + Iridium Seafoam Pudding.

[size=5][b]Fishing Expanded — 让星露谷的每一条鱼都记住你[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]中文[/b][/size]

星露谷的每一条鱼，都开始记得你——也开始对你有了评价。
Fishing Expanded 把钓鱼小游戏做成一段公平、耐玩、越钓越上头的完整生涯。不推翻原有机制，没有凭空造物，没有作弊。
它像一个真正的 DLC：先完全尊重原版钓鱼，再让它看起来本来就该是这样。

[size=4][b]核心玩法：鱼的难度等级[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]每一种鱼都有独立的难度等级（-10 ~ 100）。
[*]等级低时，鱼更弱、更容易钓；等级高时，鱼更强、奖励更好。
[*]成功钓起会涨等级，失败会掉等级。
[*][b]五大传奇鱼不参与本系统[/b]，保持原版体验；钓到一次直接给皇冠。
[/list]

[i]总之：难度动态平衡，鱼在变强，你也在变强。[/i]

[size=4][b]一次成功，鱼的难度等级怎么涨？[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]完美钓起（没脱杆）[/b]涨得最多。
[*]脱杆次数越多，涨得越少。
[*][b]玩家钓鱼等级越高，等级涨得越快[/b]（满级钓鱼等级最快）。
[*]每次成功至少会涨一点的保底。
[/list]

[size=4][b]等级带来的变化[/b][/size]

[list]

[size=4][b]皇冠：不只是收藏[/b][/size]

[list]

[*]原版 5 条传奇鱼[b]钓到一次直接给皇冠[/b]。
[*]皇冠的作用是实实在在的：[b]鱼竿手感变好[/b]。
[list]
[*]绿条更跟手、更顺滑、不拖泥带水。
[*]皇冠越多，手感越好。

[*]达到 95 级以上，再用[b]挑战鱼饵[/b]成功钓起，皇冠会升级成[b]流动金色皇冠[/b]，纯粹好看又能秀。（100 级 + 挑战鱼饵，还有彩蛋）
[/list]

[i]其他 Mod 鱼/特殊条目只显示“星星”，作为收藏；不影响手感。[/i]

[size=4][b]高难度会发生什么？[/b][/size]

高等级之后，你会遇见真正强大的鱼：理论难度可达约 5000（相对而言，原版鱼王难度一般在 100 以内）、加速更凶，甚至能整段横跳。
但奖励同步拉满——鱼获、经验、品质大幅增强。你永远不会被要求“打得更难，拿得更少”。

[list]
[*][b]鱼会跳[/b]：上蹿下跳，需要更快反应。
[*][b]鱼会累[/b]：战斗时间越长，鱼会慢慢变慢，但你的奖励不减少。
[*][b]可以停战[/b]：你 3 秒不动鱼竿，鱼会停下来休息，给你喘口气的时间。
[*][b]背板机制[/b]：同一条鱼、同一个等级，在玩家成功破招之前，行为会固定。玩家可以记招、练招。
[*][b]鱼的协助[/b]：已经收入麾下的鱼，有一定概率战斗中协助，大幅增加你的钓鱼条长度。
[*][b]挑战鱼饵 = 更难的 Boss 战[/b]：仅在高难度下，重做原版挑战鱼饵机制：
[list]
[*]鱼不再会累。不再有协助。
[*]5 分钟内钓到有额外数量加成。
[*]超时后星星会掉，鱼获变少，但[b]仍有机会拿到皇冠[/b]，不会血本无归。
[/list]
[*][b]连续失败会“放水”[/b]：高难鱼在低条区逃跑速度会变慢，给你更多熟悉机会。
[/list]

[size=4][b]失败了也有安慰奖[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]战斗超过 [b]30 秒[/b]：有概率获得 +3 钓鱼料理。
[*]战斗超过 [b]60 秒[/b]：有概率获得 [b]海泡布丁[/b]（钓鱼 +4）。
[/list]

[i]肝不会白肝，耐心有回报。[/i]

[b]巨物登场，全村围观。[/b]
高等级之后，你能钓起大到离谱的鱼：附近村民不禁赞叹（甚至包括一些特殊村民~）不过要炫耀就得趁早——一旦走进农舍，这条巨鱼就只存在于传说（和尺寸记录）里了。
