Core Gameplay: Your Reputation — Every Species Keeps Its Own Opinion of You

  • Every species keeps its own private reputation score for you, from -10 to 100.
  • Low reputation: only their plainest members bother coming out — weak, easy catches. High reputation: they line up to admire you — stronger fish, far better rewards.
  • A successful catch raises your reputation; an escape lowers it.
  • The five legendary fish stay above all of this, keeping their vanilla experience; catching one once grants a crown directly.

In short: the more they respect you, the harder they try — and the more they give back.

How Does Your Reputation Rise After a Successful Catch?

  • Perfect catches (no escapes) earn the most admiration.
  • The more escapes, the less they're impressed.
  • The higher your fishing level, the faster your reputation grows (max fishing level is fastest).
  • Every successful catch is worth at least +1 reputation.

What Changes as Your Reputation Grows

  • Harder tests: highly-rated fish become significantly harder and may use tricks like jumping.
  • More fish: catch quantities increase; at reputation 100 you can catch up to 100 at once.
  • Better quality: reputation 10 guarantees silver, reputation 25 guarantees gold, and reputation 50 guarantees iridium.
  • More XP: highly-rated fish are great for fishing mastery.
  • Bigger fish: held fish, flying animations, and displays all become larger.
  • Negative reputation: only their most ordinary members come out to spar with you — weaker fights, extra catch-bar protection, perfect for learning.

Crowns: More Than a Collection

  • Normal fish grant a crown in the collection page after a successful catch while they hold you in high regard.
  • The 5 vanilla legendary fish grant a crown directly on the first catch.
  • Crowns have a real effect: your rod handling improves.
    • The catch bar becomes more responsive, smoother, and less floaty.
    • More crowns mean better handling.
    • Countable crowns come from 56 normal vanilla fish + 5 vanilla legendary fish; collecting all 61 gives the ultimate feel.
  • Reach reputation 95+ and succeed with challenge bait to upgrade the crown into a flowing golden crown—purely gorgeous and a great flex. (At reputation 100 + challenge bait, there is even an easter egg.)

Other mod fish / special entries only display a star as a collection badge; they do not affect rod handling.

What Happens When Your Reputation Is High?

Once a species holds you in high regard, you will meet truly powerful fish: the champions they send can reach a theoretical power of about 5000 (for reference, vanilla legendary fish are usually below 100), with fiercer acceleration and even full-screen leaps.
But the rewards scale up too—catch quantity, XP, and quality all increase significantly. You will never be asked to “fight harder for less.”

  • Fish can jump: they leap up and down, requiring faster reactions.
  • Fish get tired: the longer the battle, the slower the fish becomes—but your rewards do not decrease.
  • Truce breaks: if you hold the rod still for 3 seconds, the fish stops to rest and gives you breathing room.
  • Backseeding: the same fish at the same level keeps the same behavior until you finally break it, so you can learn and practice.
  • Fish assistance: fish you have already conquered may assist you in battle, greatly extending your catch bar.
  • Challenge Bait = Harder Boss Fights: only against highly-rated fish, the vanilla challenge bait system is reworked:
    • The fish no longer gets tired. No assistance.
    • Catching within 5 minutes grants extra quantity.
    • After the time limit, stars drop and the catch shrinks, but you can still earn the crown—it is not all or nothing.
  • Consecutive failures show mercy: high-reputation fish escape more slowly in the low catch-bar zone, giving you more chances to learn.

Even Failure Has Consolation Prizes

  • Battles over 30 seconds: chance to receive a +3 fishing dish.
  • Battles over 60 seconds: chance to receive Seafoam Pudding (+4 fishing).

Your effort is never wasted; patience pays off.

A Giant Appears—The Whole Village Stares.
At high levels, you can catch absurdly huge fish. Nearby villagers will gasp in awe—each with personality-flavored praise (50+ characters and even animal friends have unique lines). But show them off early—once you enter your farmhouse, that giant fish only lives on in legend (and in the size record).

Starfruit Tea (Late-Game Bonus)

  • Once a species' reputation with you reaches 50+, successful catches have a chance to drop Starfruit Tea.
  • The higher your reputation, the higher the chance; around 25% at reputation 100.
  • Starfruit Tea permanently increases max energy—a genuinely valuable reward.

Trash, Algae, and Crab Pots

  • Trash, algae and crab pots keep score too, but their opinion of you caps at 8.
  • At opinion 8, each catch gives up to 8 items.
  • Crab pot hauls also count as a “success,” but each pot can only be collected once per day, so it is naturally limited.

Settings (Good to Know)

    • Keep default for fair festival competitions.

Quick Start Guide

  • New players: first build positive reputation with common fish while raising your fishing level.
  • Mid-game: collect crowns to improve rod handling.
  • Going for reputation 100: recommended with all crowns + max fishing level + 2x Trap Bobbers + Iridium Seafoam Pudding.
  • Want giant fish: raise a species' reputation to 8+, then parade it around town before going home.
  • Want Starfruit Tea: focus on building reputation 50+ with your favorite species.
  • Want a real challenge: equip Challenge Bait and start the backseeding boss fight.

核心玩法：你的声誉——每个鱼群都有自己的看法

  • 每一个鱼种都对你持有一份独立的评价（-10 ~ 100），这就是你在该鱼群中挣来的声誉。
  • 声誉低：出面的多是它们族里最普通的成员——鱼更弱、更容易钓；声誉高：整个鱼群都对你肃然起敬——鱼更强、奖励更好。
  • 成功钓起，你的声誉就涨一分；脱杆跑鱼，声誉就跌一分。
  • 五大传奇鱼不掺和这套评价，保持原版体验；钓到一次直接给皇冠。

总之：声誉是实打实挣来的。鱼群越尊敬你，考验就越认真——给的好处也越多。

一次成功，你的声誉怎么涨？

  • 完美钓起（没脱杆）最让它们敬佩。
  • 脱杆次数越多，它们越不买账。
  • 你的钓鱼等级越高，你的声誉涨得越快（满级钓鱼等级最快）。
  • 每次成功至少涨一点声誉，保底不落空。

声誉升高后的变化

  • 考验更难：高声誉鱼种会明显变强，还会出现“跳鱼”等花招。
  • 鱼更多：钓到的数量会变多，声誉 100 时一次最多 100 条。
  • 品质更好：声誉 10 保底银星、25 保底金星、50 保底铱星。
  • 经验更多：高声誉鱼刷钓鱼精通特别快。
  • 鱼更大：钓上来后拿在手里、飞行动画、展示都会变大。
  • 负数声誉：出面的多是族里最普通的平庸个体，专程找你练练手——鱼更弱，蓄力槽保护更强，正好当练手。

皇冠：不只是收藏

  • 普通鱼在高声誉下成功钓起后，图鉴会获得皇冠。
  • 原版 5 条传奇鱼钓到一次直接给皇冠。
  • 皇冠的作用是实实在在的：鱼竿手感变好。
    • 绿条更跟手、更顺滑、不拖泥带水。
    • 皇冠越多，手感越好。
    • 可计数皇冠来自 56 种普通原生鱼 + 5 条原版传奇鱼，集齐这 61 个 = 完全体手感。
  • 声誉达到 95 以上，再用挑战鱼饵成功钓起，皇冠会升级成流动金色皇冠，纯粹好看又能秀。（声誉 100 + 挑战鱼饵，还有彩蛋）

其他 Mod 鱼/特殊条目只显示“星星”，作为收藏；不影响手感。

声誉很高时会发生什么？

当一个鱼种对你评价极高，你会遇见真正强大的对手：它们派出的精锐战力理论上可达约 5000（相对而言，原版鱼王一般在 100 以内）、加速更凶，甚至能整段横跳。
但奖励同步拉满——鱼获、经验、品质大幅增强。你永远不会被要求“打得更难，拿得更少”。

  • 鱼会跳：上蹿下跳，需要更快反应。
  • 鱼会累：战斗时间越长，鱼会慢慢变慢，但你的奖励不减少。
  • 可以停战：你 3 秒不动鱼竿，鱼会停下来休息，给你喘口气的时间。
  • 背板机制：同一种鱼、同一档声誉，在你成功破招之前，行为会固定。可以记招、练招。
  • 鱼的协助：已经收入麾下的鱼，有一定概率战斗中协助，大幅增加你的钓鱼条长度。
  • 挑战鱼饵 = 更难的 Boss 战：仅对高声誉的鱼，重做原版挑战鱼饵机制：
    • 鱼不再会累。不再有协助。
    • 5 分钟内钓到有额外数量加成。
    • 超时后星星会掉，鱼获变少，但仍有机会拿到皇冠，不会血本无归。
  • 连续失败会“放水”：高声誉的鱼在低条区逃跑速度会变慢，给你更多熟悉机会。

失败了也有安慰奖

  • 战斗超过 60 秒：有概率获得 海泡布丁（钓鱼 +4）。
