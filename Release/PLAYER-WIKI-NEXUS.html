Fishing Expanded — Every Fish in Stardew Valley Will Remember You

English

Every fish in Stardew Valley is starting to remember you—and form an opinion about you.
Fishing Expanded turns the fishing minigame into a fair, deep, and endlessly addictive career. It doesn't overturn vanilla mechanics, doesn't add fake items, and doesn't cheat.
It feels like a true DLC: it respects vanilla fishing first, then makes it look like this is exactly how the game was always meant to be.

Core Gameplay: Fish Reputation — Every Species Rates You

  • Every species keeps its own independent opinion of you, from -10 to 100 — your reputation within that fish population.
  • Low reputation: they barely take you seriously — weak, easy catches. High reputation: they start testing you properly — stronger fish, far better rewards.
  • A successful catch raises their opinion of you; an escape lowers it.
  • The five legendary fish stand above all opinions, keeping their vanilla experience; catching one once grants a crown directly.

In short: respect is earned dynamically. The more a species respects you, the harder it tests you — and the more it gives back.

How Does Their Opinion of You Rise After a Successful Catch?

  • Perfect catches (no escapes) impress them the most.
  • The more escapes, the less impressed they are.
  • The higher your fishing level, the faster their opinion of you grows (max fishing level is fastest).
  • Every successful catch is worth at least +1 respect.

What Changes as Their Opinion of You Grows

  • Harder tests: highly-rated fish become significantly harder and may use tricks like jumping.
  • More fish: catch quantities increase; at opinion 100 you can catch up to 100 at once.
  • Better quality: opinion 10 guarantees silver, opinion 25 guarantees gold, and opinion 50 guarantees iridium.
  • More XP: highly-rated fish are great for fishing mastery.
  • Bigger fish: held fish, flying animations, and displays all become larger.
  • Negative reputation: fish go easy on you and the catch bar gets more protection, great for learning.

Crowns: More Than a Collection

  • Normal fish grant a crown in the collection page after a successful catch while their opinion of you is high.
  • The 5 vanilla legendary fish grant a crown directly on the first catch.
  • Crowns have a real effect: your rod handling improves.
    • The catch bar becomes more responsive, smoother, and less floaty.
    • More crowns mean better handling.
    • Countable crowns come from 56 normal vanilla fish + 5 vanilla legendary fish; collecting all 61 gives the ultimate feel.
  • Reach opinion 95+ and succeed with challenge bait to upgrade the crown into a flowing golden crown—purely gorgeous and a great flex. (At opinion 100 + challenge bait, there is even an easter egg.)

Other mod fish / special entries only display a star as a collection badge; they do not affect rod handling.

What Happens When Your Reputation Is High?

Once a species rates you highly, you will meet truly powerful fish: the champions they send can reach a theoretical power of about 5000 (for reference, vanilla legendary fish are usually below 100), with fiercer acceleration and even full-screen leaps.
But the rewards scale up too—catch quantity, XP, and quality all increase significantly. You will never be asked to “fight harder for less.”

  • Fish can jump: they leap up and down, requiring faster reactions.
  • Fish get tired: the longer the battle, the slower the fish becomes—but your rewards do not decrease.
  • Truce breaks: if you hold the rod still for 3 seconds, the fish stops to rest and gives you breathing room.
  • Backseeding: the same fish at the same level keeps the same behavior until you finally break it, so you can learn and practice.
  • Fish assistance: fish you have already conquered may assist you in battle, greatly extending your catch bar.
  • Challenge Bait = Harder Boss Fights: only at high difficulty, the vanilla challenge bait system is reworked:
    • The fish no longer gets tired. No assistance.
    • Catching within 5 minutes grants extra quantity.
    • After the time limit, stars drop and the catch shrinks, but you can still earn the crown—it is not all or nothing.
  • Consecutive failures show mercy: high-reputation fish escape more slowly in the low catch-bar zone, giving you more chances to learn.

Even Failure Has Consolation Prizes

  • Battles over 30 seconds: chance to receive a +3 fishing dish.
  • Battles over 60 seconds: chance to receive Seafoam Pudding (+4 fishing).

Your effort is never wasted; patience pays off.

A Giant Appears—The Whole Village Stares.
At high levels, you can catch absurdly huge fish. Nearby villagers will gasp in awe (even some special villagers~). But show them off early—once you enter your farmhouse, that giant fish only lives on in legend (and in the size record).

Starfruit Tea (Late-Game Bonus)

  • Once a species' opinion of you reaches 50+, successful catches have a chance to drop Starfruit Tea.
  • The higher their opinion, the higher the chance; around 25% at opinion 100.
  • Starfruit Tea permanently increases max energy—a genuinely valuable reward.

Trash, Algae, and Crab Pots

  • Trash, algae and crab pots rate you too, but their opinion caps at 8.
  • At opinion 8, each catch gives up to 8 items.
  • Crab pot hauls also count as a “success,” but each pot can only be collected once per day, so it is naturally limited.

Settings (Good to Know)

  • Works out of the box with no required configuration.
  • Festival fishing is fully vanilla by default:
    • Keep default for fair festival competitions.
    • Enable mod rules in GMCM if you want to use the mod to score higher.
  • Mod logging can be enabled (useful for bug reports).
  • Supports resetting all challenge progress.
  • Supports custom rank titles; edit the config file manually (not in GMCM).

Quick Start Guide

  • New players: first build positive reputation with common fish while raising your fishing level.
  • Mid-game: collect crowns to improve rod handling.
  • Going for reputation 100: recommended with all crowns + max fishing level + 2x Trap Bobbers + Iridium Seafoam Pudding.
  • Want giant fish: raise a species' opinion to 8+, then parade it around town before going home.
  • Want Starfruit Tea: focus on building opinion 50+ with your favorite species.
  • Want a real challenge: equip Challenge Bait and start the backseeding boss fight.

Fishing Expanded — 让星露谷的每一条鱼都记住你

中文

星露谷的每一条鱼，都开始记得你——也开始对你有了评价。
Fishing Expanded 把钓鱼小游戏做成一段公平、耐玩、越钓越上头的完整生涯。不推翻原有机制，没有凭空造物，没有作弊。
它像一个真正的 DLC：先完全尊重原版钓鱼，再让它看起来本来就该是这样。

核心玩法：鱼群评价——每个种群都在给你打分

  • 每一个鱼种都对你持有一份独立的评价（-10 ~ 100），这就是你在该鱼群中的声誉。
  • 评价低：它们没把你当回事——鱼更弱、更容易钓；评价高：它们开始认真考你——鱼更强、奖励更好。
  • 成功钓起会让它们更看得起你（评价上涨），脱杆则会让评价下跌。
  • 五大传奇鱼超然于一切评价之外，保持原版体验；钓到一次直接给皇冠。

总之：尊重是动态挣来的。一个鱼种越看得起你，就越要认真考你——给的也越多。

一次成功，鱼群的评价怎么涨？

  • 完美钓起（没脱杆）最让它们刮目相看。
  • 脱杆次数越多，它们越不为所动。
  • 你的钓鱼等级越高，它们对你的评价涨得越快（满级钓鱼等级最快）。
  • 每次成功至少涨一点尊重，保底不落空。

评价升高后的变化

  • 考验更难：高评价鱼种会明显变强，还会出现“跳鱼”等花招。
  • 鱼更多：钓到的数量会变多，评价 100 时一次最多 100 条。
  • 品质更好：评价 10 保底银星、25 保底金星、50 保底铱星。
  • 经验更多：高评价鱼刷钓鱼精通特别快。
  • 鱼更大：钓上来后拿在手里、飞行动画、展示都会变大。
  • 负数评价：鱼会放水，蓄力槽保护更强，适合新手练手。

皇冠：不只是收藏

  • 普通鱼在高难度成功钓起后，图鉴会获得皇冠。
  • 原版 5 条传奇鱼钓到一次直接给皇冠。
  • 皇冠的作用是实实在在的：鱼竿手感变好。
    • 绿条更跟手、更顺滑、不拖泥带水。
    • 皇冠越多，手感越好。
    • 可计数皇冠来自 56 种普通原生鱼 + 5 条原版传奇鱼，集齐这 61 个 = 完全体手感。
  • 评价达到 95 以上，再用挑战鱼饵成功钓起，皇冠会升级成流动金色皇冠，纯粹好看又能秀。（评价 100 + 挑战鱼饵，还有彩蛋）

其他 Mod 鱼/特殊条目只显示“星星”，作为收藏；不影响手感。

声誉很高时会发生什么？

当一个鱼种对你评价极高，你会遇见真正强大的对手：它们派出的战力理论上可达约 5000（相对而言，原版鱼王一般在 100 以内）、加速更凶，甚至能整段横跳。
但奖励同步拉满——鱼获、经验、品质大幅增强。你永远不会被要求“打得更难，拿得更少”。

  • 鱼会跳：上蹿下跳，需要更快反应。
  • 鱼会累：战斗时间越长，鱼会慢慢变慢，但你的奖励不减少。
  • 可以停战：你 3 秒不动鱼竿，鱼会停下来休息，给你喘口气的时间。
  • 背板机制：同一种鱼、同一档评价，在你成功破招之前，行为会固定。可以记招、练招。
  • 鱼的协助：已经收入麾下的鱼，有一定概率战斗中协助，大幅增加你的钓鱼条长度。
  • 挑战鱼饵 = 更难的 Boss 战：仅对高评价的鱼，重做原版挑战鱼饵机制：
    • 鱼不再会累。不再有协助。
    • 5 分钟内钓到有额外数量加成。
    • 超时后星星会掉，鱼获变少，但仍有机会拿到皇冠，不会血本无归。
  • 连续失败会“放水”：高评价的鱼在低条区逃跑速度会变慢，给你更多熟悉机会。

失败了也有安慰奖

  • 战斗超过 30 秒：有概率获得 +3 钓鱼料理。
  • 战斗超过 60 秒：有概率获得 海泡布丁（钓鱼 +4）。

肝不会白肝，耐心有回报。

巨物登场，全村围观。
高等级之后，你能钓起大到离谱的鱼：附近村民不禁赞叹（甚至包括一些特殊村民~）不过要炫耀就得趁早——一旦走进农舍，这条巨鱼就只存在于传说（和尺寸记录）里了。

星之果茶（后期福利）

  • 某个鱼种对你的评价 ≥50 后，成功钓起有几率获得星之果茶。
  • 评价越高概率越高，评价 100 时约 25%。
  • 星之果茶可以永久提高体力上限，是实打实的好东西。

垃圾、藻类、蟹笼

  • 垃圾、藻类和蟹笼也会评价你，但封顶 8。
  • 评价到 8 后每次最多拿 8 个。
  • 蟹笼收获也算“成功”，但每个笼子每天只能收一次，天然有限。

设置（建议知道）

  • 默认开箱即玩，不用改任何设置。
  • 节日钓鱼默认完全原版：
    • 想公平比赛，保持默认。
    • 想用 Mod 规则冲高分，可以在 GMCM 里开启。
  • 可以开启 Mod 日志（遇到 bug 使用）。
  • 支持重置全部挑战进度。
  • 支持自定义称号，仅支持编辑 config 文件修改。

快速上手指南

  • 新手期：先让常见鱼对你有正评价，顺便练高钓鱼等级。
  • 中后期：收集皇冠，提升鱼竿手感。
  • 想冲评价 100：建议全皇冠 + 钓鱼等级满级 + 2*陷阱浮标 + 铱星海泡布丁。
  • 想看巨型鱼：把某个鱼种的评价刷到 8 以上，钓到后别急着进家门，举着去人多的地方。
  • 想刷星之果茶：优先把喜欢的鱼种刷到评价 50 以上。
  • 想挑战自我：换上挑战鱼饵，开始背板 Boss 战。

一句话总结

这个 Mod 不是让你更肝，而是让每一次抛竿都有成长、有惊喜、有故事。

它们对你的评价越来越高，你也越来越强。下一杆，可能就是你封神的那一杆。
